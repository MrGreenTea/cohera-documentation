# Open Questions

Conceptual questions for the design direction of Cohera.

## Target Audience & Onboarding

1. Who is our primary target group — hobbyists, students, professionals, or others?
2. Do we expect every community to have a developer, or should non-technical people be able to set up a platform too?
3. How easy can we make it for new users, especially civil society organizations, to start their platform?
4. Should we lean into opinionated starter kits for common community types instead of relying on module composition?

## Community & Identity Model

5. How do we model people belonging to multiple groups — does each Cohera instance represent a single community?
6. Can one instance host multiple communities, or do users have accounts across separate instances?
7. What is the identity model across federated instances — do users have separate accounts per instance?
8. Is there a cross-instance identity concept?

## Governance

9. How do communities govern themselves beyond admin/moderator/member roles?

## Federation

10. Is federation a core value or an optional feature?
11. What does federation mean for communities of 20–200 people?
12. Is ActivityPub actually suitable for small, trust-based communities?
13. Is there a simpler inter-community sharing model than ActivityPub?
14. How do we moderate federated content?

## Module Design

15. Should modules include routes/pages, or only components?
16. What should the right set of first-party modules be?
17. How do we decide which modules (like voting, shared docs, finances, tasks) are in scope?

## Portability & Scale

18. Can communities migrate to a different platform?
19. What happens when a community grows beyond 200 people?

## Hosting & Sustainability

20. Who hosts these platforms — are managed hosting or community hosting cooperatives part of the vision?
21. What is the sustainability model (grant-funded, cooperative, etc.)?
